"Graphic Novel: Motherhood in Crisis"

These stories all show how quickly life can change over circumstances that are beyond that person's control. Of course they all have a similar theme of motherhood and the struggles of taking care of children when poor, but each of the stories are very different in circumstances. The first story about Memunnatu is about the challenges of pregnancy, which is a very taxing situation to be in for the body. She was constantly suffering for many weeks before she was even taken to the hospital, and even after going to the hospital it was still very tough for her to keep going. She required 24/7 care from the nurses before and after her operation. It was also a new environment for her with a lot of strangers, which also took some time getting used to. Eventually she gave birth to her child and then went back to her home. Still she was scared to go back to that center because of the trauma caused by the pain she suffered. Unfortunately the center would close down due to Covid and this would be one way that lives were impacted from external factors.
In the second story, Heela has to deal with the challenges of rape and unwanted pregnancies. Heela wanted to be a nurse for her whole life and worked hard in schooll despite many unfortunate circumstances she was in. She had lost her mother from ebola and had to live with her grandmother after her father remarried with another woman. Things would get worse for her when one day she met a 18 year old man who asked her to be his girlfriend. When he got rejected, he started to become more forceful with his requests and demanded her to come to his house, where they would sleep together. Heela didn't want to do this but had no choice, and this is what led to her forced pregnancy. She didn't realize that she was pregnant at first, and this is probably the saddest part of the story. How her dreams of being a nurse were taken away from her forcefully and now she has to take care of her kid, and while she is optimistic for her new life, it still sad that she couldn't have her own future. The man who did this to her still hasn't been found either which shows the lack of justice in her area.
In the third story, Kaidatu constantly gave birth to children that would shortly die right after. She was seen by many in her village as a witch that could only produce children that couldn't live for long. For her most recent child, she was forced to be taken to the hospital, where she had many different operations performed on her. It wasn't certain if she could even live if she gave birth to that child. Luckily Kaidatu managed to surivve after months of treaments with her baby alive as well. However once again the hospital that helped with this would close down due to Covid.
In the last story, Tetteh was in an abusive relationship with her husband, who gave her a lot of beatings due to gambling issues. This eventually led to her having to protect herself with a knife, where she accidentally killed him. Tetteh was sent to prison while pregnant, and suffered for most of her time there, with very little help from the nurses and medicine. Eventually she had to give birth and the child she gave birth to couldn't stay with her long because of her sentence. Now she awaits for the chance to see her son again. These stories show many circumstances that either weren't in the women's control or they were forced into circumstances they didn't want to be in. With it either being because of covid, abuse, or the justice system, this shows how the world we live in can still use more help for these struggling villages and people.
