My vision for this eyepiece was determination and seeing a possibility. Firstly the eye, I kinda made it look a normal eye on the outside, but the pupil has a firey look to it. I based this off a scene from the anime Jojo's Bizarre Adventure called dark determination. I made my eye show a moment of realization in the moment. The background is also meant to be somewhat empty space, to signal how the eye is focused on one thing. The stars are also meant to show sparks of realization as well.
