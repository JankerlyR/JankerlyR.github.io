My Photomontage is about the difference between AI and Humans in work productivity between them. My main goal was to show how AI is progessing evolving to work faster and smarter than humans. Humans on the other hand have started to become lazier with the use of AI, showing the unemployment that a lot of people face nowadays due to the job market. My photomontage was arranged to show how AI are currently above humans in the battle of productivity, showing a light of inspiration near them. Humans are the other hand are slowly turning their brain into mush and becoming more and more lazier.
In Photoshop, I used 5 different images each with their own adjustment layer, on top of another adjustment layer to create a light and dark area for the photos. The light area at the bottom symbolizes the good in humans if they can work hard, but not being there yet. The dark area at the top symbolizes AI and the terrible implications they may have on the job market, if companies abuse them. I used Screen, Darken Color, and Lighten blend modes on the images with around 70% opacity on each of them. This was done to show the light and dark aspects of the images as previously mentioned. Lastly, All of my images came from either adobe stock or wikimedia commons.
